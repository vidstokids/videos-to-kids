Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Who we are

Vids to Kids is an independent creator producing children's videos. You can reach us at vidstokids@gmail.com.

What our app does

Our app "Make (vids to kids)" is a private automation used only by the Vids to Kids team. With our own consent, it accesses our own Google account to generate voiceovers with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, read and update our spreadsheets, store files in our Google Drive and upload videos to our YouTube channel.

What data we collect

The app does not collect, store or share personal data from anyone outside our team. It does not sign in members of the public and does not use data for advertising.

Google user data

Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to run our own video production and is never sold or transferred to third parties, except the automation service (Make) that runs the workflow on our behalf. Our use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings under Security, Third-party apps with account access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
